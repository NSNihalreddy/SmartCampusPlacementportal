BUILT FOR YOUR ROLE

How PLACEHUB Helps You

A dedicated experience for students preparing for placements and faculty managing campus recruitment.

For Students

Manage your placement journey with a clear view of opportunities, applications and progress.

  • Explore available companies, job roles and placement opportunities.
  • View eligibility requirements before applying for a drive.
  • Apply for suitable opportunities through your student portal.
  • Maintain your profile and upload or update your resume.
  • Follow application status and interview progress.
  • Receive placement announcements and relevant notifications.

For Faculty

Coordinate placement activities more efficiently and keep student recruitment information organized.

  • Access and manage student placement records according to your role.
  • Organize company information and placement opportunities.
  • Review student applications and recruitment progress.
  • Monitor shortlisting, interviews and selection outcomes.
  • Share important announcements with students and faculty.
  • View placement reports to understand overall progress.
